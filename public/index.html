      How to play
      • Andolankari — walk into people to recruit them. W / click NAARA pulls in people nearby; every 10 people shouting together leave one policeman dizzy. You need 10+ people to break a barricade: hold A PUSH. 10 of your people around a policeman push him out. 5 of your people at a police van unlock it and free one prisoner; 10 tip the van over and free everyone. S CHHUDAO speeds up a rescue. D dash. Sit inside the dashed circle on Jantar Mantar Road with 5+ people until the meter fills.
      • Police — click LATHI scatters the crowd. Every 10 people you hit earn one backup constable: W CALL (up to 5). You start with 5 tear gas shells; every 5 people you hit earn 3 more. A GAS scatters up to 5 people, or leaves a lone person dizzy for 10 seconds. S DRAG grabs one protester; vans stop for 10 seconds when you bring someone close — press S at the van to put them in. With 5 police together, E BARRIER repairs a broken barricade or puts up a new one across the road. D dash.
      • Rounds last 5 minutes online. Andolankari win if any kafila completes the dharna; police win if time runs out.
      • Phone: drag the joystick on the left to walk, buttons on the right. Computer: point with the mouse or use the arrow keys.

      Satire. Every kafila, slogan and player name is fictional; the map is a simplified cartoon of central New Delhi, not a navigation map. 🤖 = AI player.
